Frequently asked questions

How much does a website cost?

A focused landing page starts from €450, a business website from €850 and a more advanced premium website from €1,400. The final quote depends on content, functionality and timeline.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I am based in Vienna, but the full design and development process can be handled remotely for clients worldwide. Communication is available in English and German.

Is SEO included?

Every project includes an SEO-ready technical foundation: semantic structure, metadata, mobile optimisation and basic indexing files. Ongoing content strategy, link building and paid advertising are separate services.

How long does a website take?

A landing page commonly takes around one to three weeks. Larger websites take longer depending on feedback speed, content readiness and required functionality.
